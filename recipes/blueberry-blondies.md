## Blueberry Blondies / Blauwe Bosbes Blondies

i had some blueberries in the fridge i needed to use

## Ingredients / Ingrediënten

### Blueberry layer / Bosbeslaag
- **500 g** blueberries (blauwe bosbessen)
- **80 g** sugar (suiker)
- **10 g** lemon juice (citroensap)
- **10 g** cornstarch (maïzena)

### Blondie batter / Blondiebeslag
- **280 g** unsalted butter (ongezouten boter)
- **300 g** sugar, white or light brown (suiker)
- **3 large eggs** (~180 g without shells) (eieren)
- **1 egg yolk** (~20 g) (eidooier)
- **12 g** vanilla extract (vanille‑extract)
- **6 g** salt (zout)
- **260 g** all‑purpose flour (tarwebloem)

---

## Method / Werkwijze

### 1. Concentrate the blueberries
Put the blueberries, sugar, and lemon juice in a saucepan over medium heat.
The blueberries go in **raw**, not dried or pre-treated. As they heat, they will
release their own liquid. The lemon juice is not meant to add bulk liquid; it
adds acidity to help the fruit break down and brighten flavor.

Cook 8–10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until most berries have burst and
the mixture looks loose but glossy. Dissolve the cornstarch in a spoon of cold
water, stir it in, and cook 1 minute more until thick, jam-like, and able to
hold its shape briefly when dragged with a spoon. Remove from heat and let cool
completely. This step removes excess water so the blondies set fudgy instead of
wet.

### 2. Prepare the pan
Heat the oven to **175 °C**. Line a 9×13 pan with baking paper, leaving an
overhang on the long sides. This makes lifting and slicing easy once cooled.

### 3. Melt the butter
Melt the butter gently in a saucepan or microwave until just liquid. Do not
brown it here; you want clean butter flavor and tight crumb. Let it cool until
warm but not hot.

### 4. Build the sugar base
In a large bowl, combine the melted butter and sugar. Stir until fully mixed
and slightly glossy. This creates the dense brownie‑style structure.

### 5. Add eggs and flavor
Stir in the eggs one at a time, then the extra yolk, mixing well after each.
Add vanilla and salt. The batter should look thick, smooth, and cohesive, not
foamy.

### 6. Fold in the flour
Add the flour all at once and fold gently until just combined. Stop as soon as
you no longer see dry flour. Overmixing here makes the bars tough instead of
chewy.

### 7. Assemble
Spread about **two‑thirds** of the batter evenly in the pan. Spoon the cooled
blueberry mixture over the surface in blobs and streaks. Dollop the remaining
batter on top and lightly swirl with a knife. Do not fully mix — distinct
layers keep the structure intact.

### 8. Bake
Bake at **175 °C** for **35–45 minutes**. The top should be set and lightly
golden, and a skewer inserted should come out with moist crumbs but no liquid
jam. The center will firm up as it cools.

### 9. Cool completely
Let the blondies cool fully in the pan, at least 2 hours. This is non‑negotiable
for clean slices and proper texture.

---

## Freezing / Invriezen

Slice once fully cooled. Wrap individual pieces tightly and freeze flat. These
freeze extremely well and thaw chewy and dense, like good brownies. Best eaten
at room temperature after thawing.
